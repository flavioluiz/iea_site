Space Program

ITA plays a leading role in developing space technologies in Brazil. The the Division concentrates expertise in space propulsion, launch vehicles, satellites, guidance, navigation and control (GNC) systems, and reentry technologies — in collaboration with DCTA, AEB, INPE, and international partners.

Why Space?

Space dominance is strategic for Brazil. IEA/ITA trains the engineers and researchers who develop the technologies needed for the country to achieve space autonomy — from satellite design to the development of national launch vehicles.

Our Focus Areas

Space Propulsion

Solid and liquid propellant engines, electric propulsion, micropropulsion systems for CubeSats.

Satellites and CubeSats

Design, integration, and operation of small satellites. Participation in national and international missions.

Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC)

Attitude and orbit control algorithms, inertial navigation, space sensors.

Launch Vehicles

Rocket aerodynamics, launcher structures, stage separation systems.

Remote Sensing

Satellite instrumentation, orbital image processing, environmental and defense applications.

Atmospheric Reentry

High-temperature thermodynamics, thermal shields, reentry ballistics.

Space Research Lines

Rocket and Space Propulsion

Hybrid and liquid/solid-propellant rocket engines, thrust chambers, combustion instability, and satellite thrusters. Covers the L75 LOx-ethanol engine and sounding rockets.

Hypersonic Vehicles and Atmospheric Reentry

Hypersonic flows, shock wave/boundary layer interaction, thermal protection, ablation, and reentry ballistics. Covers hypersonic civilian and military vehicles, missiles, and spacecraft reentry.

Orbital Mechanics and Astrodynamics

Orbital trajectories, transfers, ballistic capture, and lunar missions. Covers the N-body problem and astrodynamics applied to Brazilian missions such as GARATÉA-L.

Satellites and Space Systems

Satellite and CubeSat design, space subsystems (ADCS, EPS, OBC, TT&C), attitude and orbit control, space instrumentation, and nanosatellite missions.

Satellite Launch Vehicles

Design, guidance, navigation and control (GNC), flight dynamics, and trajectory optimization of satellite launch vehicles. Covers suborbital and orbital vehicles, stage separation, systems engineering, and nano-launchers.

Space Operations and Systems

Launch operations, range safety, space vehicle tracking and surveillance, TT&C systems, space system lifecycle, and mission management. Includes activities related to the Alcântara Space Center and the ITA Space Center.

Description

The ITA Space Center (CEI) is the hub for space activities at the Technological Institute of Aeronautics. It integrates research, teaching, and technological development in satellites, launch vehicles, space operations, and tracking systems. It houses the ITASAT program and projects in partnership with the Brazilian Air Force and international space agencies.

Infrastructure

  • Clean room for satellite integration
  • Tracking and TT&C systems
  • Space operations simulators (SOLIS/STK)
  • Space embedded systems laboratory
  • Satellite subsystem test environment

Description

The Rocket Technologies Laboratory (LTF) is dedicated to research and development in rocket propulsion systems, solid and hybrid propellant rocket motors, thruster test benches, and technologies associated with launch vehicles. It supports R&D projects with emphasis on space propulsion and atmospheric sounding.

Infrastructure

  • Rocket motor test benches
  • Propulsion data acquisition systems
  • Solid and hybrid propellant equipment
  • Flame diagnostic instrumentation
