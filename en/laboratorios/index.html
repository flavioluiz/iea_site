Laboratories

PG-EAM has modern laboratory infrastructure for research and technological development in Aeronautical and Mechanical Engineering. Our laboratories are equipped with cutting-edge technology to meet the demands of academic projects and industry partnerships.

The laboratories are distributed among the three concentration areas of the program and offer resources for experimental testing, computational simulations, materials characterization, advanced manufacturing, and systems development.

Description

The Dynamics and Vibrations Laboratory of the Mechanical Engineering Division at ITA was founded in 2015. It hosts teaching and research activities related to the dynamics of rigid and flexible bodies. The laboratory provides experimental classes in Mechanical Vibrations, as well as research in modal analysis, structural dynamics, and vibratory systems.

Infrastructure

  • 2kN electrodynamic shaker
  • Instrumented impact hammer
  • Brüel & Kjær triaxial accelerometers
  • National Instruments acquisition system
  • LabView software
  • Smart structures equipment

The Aerospace Structures Laboratory (LAB-ESP) primarily hosts experimental and numerical research activities in Solid Mechanics and Structures. The...

Description

The Aerospace Structures Laboratory (LAB-ESP) primarily hosts experimental and numerical research activities in Solid Mechanics and Structures. The physical area consists of an 800 m² hangar equipped with adequate conditions for testing medium and large aeronautical structures. The laboratory aims to provide qualified personnel and adequate infrastructure for planning and executing structural tests and characterization of composite and metallic materials.

Infrastructure

  • Universal testing machines
  • Data acquisition system
  • Static test equipment
  • Experimental stress analysis
  • Dynamic and vibration testing
  • Aeroelastic testing
  • Additive manufacturing of composites
  • Finite element numerical tools

The Hydraulic Machines Laboratory has hydraulic turbine benches of Pelton, Francis, Kaplan types and centrifugal pump for cavitation studies and axial pump cavitation study circuit. It also has complete instrumentation including manometers, Pitot tubes, and a computerized acquisition system based on LabView.

Infrastructure

  • Francis turbine bench
  • Kaplan turbine bench
  • Centrifugal pump for cavitation
  • Prony brakes
  • Stroboscope
  • LabView system

Description

The ITA Manufacturing Competence Center (CCM/ITA) is a teaching and research environment for the Integrated Product Development concept. It occupies an area of 650 m² and was designed to provide students with the opportunity to visualize and understand the integral product and system development process. It works in 5-axis milling, metallic additive manufacturing, industrial automation, robotics, and inspection.

Infrastructure

  • 5-axis CNC machining centers
  • KUKA industrial robots (KR500, KR210)
  • K-SERIES metrology systems
  • Laser Tracker
  • iGPS
  • SLM printer (additive manufacturing)
  • Residual stress measurement equipment
