Research Lines

Research at ITA’s the Division is organized into thematic lines covering the full spectrum of aeronautical and aerospace engineering, with growing emphasis on space technologies.

Aerodynamics and Aeroacoustics

Study of aerodynamic flows, aeroacoustic noise generation and control, including jet noise, trailing-edge noise, and hydrodynamic sources. Encompasses computational and experimental methods.

105 pubs 34 TGs 24 theses

Flow Stability and Transition to Turbulence

Analysis of hydrodynamic instabilities, boundary layer control, transition to turbulence, and turbulence modeling. Includes shock-laden flows relevant to high-speed vehicles.

52 pubs 5 TGs 10 theses

Aeroelasticity and Structural Dynamics

Fluid-structure interaction phenomena, flutter, divergence, aeroservoelasticity, and nonlinear dynamics in aerospace structures. Includes experimental identification (GVT, VCT) and reduced-order models.

88 pubs 22 TGs 38 theses

Flight Mechanics and Control

Flight dynamics of rigid and flexible aircraft, active, passive, and adaptive control laws. Includes spacecraft attitude control, GNC, and Port-Hamiltonian-based systems.

58 pubs 71 TGs 23 theses

Solid Mechanics and Aerospace Materials

Mechanical behavior of aerospace materials: composites, alloys, ceramics, and smart materials. Covers fracture, fatigue, damage, micromechanics, additive manufacturing, and experimental characterization.

148 pubs 69 TGs 67 theses

Aerospace Vehicle Structures

Stability, buckling, and optimization of aircraft and launcher structures. Covers stiffened panels and shells, knock-down factors, VCT, and multidisciplinary structural design.

50 pubs 56 TGs 41 theses

Numerical and Computational Methods

Development and application of high-order numerical methods for simulation of aerospace flows and structures. Covers spectral elements, discontinuous Galerkin, dispersion-diffusion analysis, and reduced-order models.

64 pubs 27 TGs 59 theses

Rocket and Space Propulsion

Hybrid and liquid/solid-propellant rocket engines, thrust chambers, combustion instability, and satellite thrusters. Covers the L75 LOx-ethanol engine and sounding rockets.

17 pubs 36 TGs 16 theses

Aircraft Propulsion and Fuels

Gas turbines, turbofans, propellers, and distributed propulsion. Covers sustainable aviation fuels (SAF/biojet), aviation kerosene, and performance of aeronautical propulsion systems.

14 pubs 10 TGs 5 theses

Electric Propulsion and Urban Air Mobility

Electric and hybrid-electric propulsion for aircraft, fuel cells (PEMFC), hydrogen aircraft, and urban air mobility. Covers performance and exergy analysis of clean propulsion systems.

14 pubs 3 TGs 2 theses

Hypersonic Vehicles and Atmospheric Reentry

Hypersonic flows, shock wave/boundary layer interaction, thermal protection, ablation, and reentry ballistics. Covers hypersonic civilian and military vehicles, missiles, and spacecraft reentry.

3 pubs 24 TGs 5 theses

Orbital Mechanics and Astrodynamics

Orbital trajectories, transfers, ballistic capture, and lunar missions. Covers the N-body problem and astrodynamics applied to Brazilian missions such as GARATÉA-L.

52 pubs 29 TGs 4 theses

Satellites and Space Systems

Satellite and CubeSat design, space subsystems (ADCS, EPS, OBC, TT&C), attitude and orbit control, space instrumentation, and nanosatellite missions.

8 pubs 25 TGs

Multidisciplinary Design of Aircraft and Space Vehicles

Conceptual and preliminary design of aircraft, launchers, and space vehicles. Covers multidisciplinary design optimization (MDO), UAVs, performance analysis, and applied aerodynamics.

11 pubs 97 TGs 16 theses

Satellite Launch Vehicles

Design, guidance, navigation and control (GNC), flight dynamics, and trajectory optimization of satellite launch vehicles. Covers suborbital and orbital vehicles, stage separation, systems engineering, and nano-launchers.

3 pubs 9 TGs

Space Operations and Systems

Launch operations, range safety, space vehicle tracking and surveillance, TT&C systems, space system lifecycle, and mission management. Includes activities related to the Alcântara Space Center and the ITA Space Center.

2 pubs 8 TGs 1 theses
