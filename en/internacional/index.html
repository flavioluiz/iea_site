International

PG-EAM prioritizes internationalization as a strategy to maintain its national reference status and expand its international recognition as a center of excellence in education, research, and innovation in the aerospace sector.

International Impact

The program has significantly expanded its international cooperations:

  • 34.5% of publications with international co-authorship (45% increase in the last four years)
  • 87.1% of citations from abroad
  • ~50% of courses available in English
