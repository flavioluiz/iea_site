Research Projects

The Graduate Program in Aeronautical and Mechanical Engineering develops research projects in partnership with companies, funding agencies, and international institutions. These projects are essential for laboratory funding, human resource training, and the production of high-impact scientific and technological knowledge.

The projects listed below are ongoing and involve permanent and collaborating faculty of the program, as well as master’s, doctoral, and postdoctoral students.
